3. Alternatively, click **"Register a new company tenant"** to create your own isolated multi-tenant environment.

## Core Features & Workflows

### 1. Managing the Workflow Pipeline (Kanban)
* View all active tasks assigned across your workspace.
* Monitor live operational Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) at the top of the dashboard (Total Active Tasks, In Progress, and Completed).
* Transition task states dynamically using the dropdown in the action column (*Backlog* $\rightarrow$ *In Progress* $\rightarrow$ *In Review* $\rightarrow$ *Done*).

### 2. Creating Workflow Tasks
* Click the **"+ Create New Task"** button in the header.
* Fill out the task details:
  - **Task Title & Description:** Clear deliverables and requirements.
  - **Assigned Department / Role:** Route tasks to specialized roles (Business Analyst, Systems Engineer, Database Administrator, Executive Management).
  - **Priority:** Set urgency levels (*Normal*, *High*, *Critical*).
* Click **Save Task** to instantly append it to your tenant's operational pipeline.

### 3. Auditing & Governance Trail
* Navigate to the **"Audit Logs & History"** tab from the top navigation bar.
* Review real-time, immutable, and timestamped logs tracking every event within your tenant (including user logins, task creations, status transitions, and data modifications) for governance and compliance.

### 4. Session Security & Tenant Isolation
* Use the **"Sign Out"** button to securely terminate your active session.
* Tenant data is strictly isolated by company credentials, ensuring robust multi-tenant data privacy.
